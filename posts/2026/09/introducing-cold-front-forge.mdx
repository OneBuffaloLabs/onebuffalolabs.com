When I first opened up the workshop under the name **1BfloPrints** earlier this year, it was designed as an informal outlet: a catch-all repository for test prints, small replacement brackets, and Creality K2 SE upgrades.

Over the past several months, that small experiment grew into something far more structured. We weren't just printing random one-offs anymore; we were engineering functional product lines, writing clean parametric CAD in OpenSCAD, and fulfilling custom physical orders.

We outgrew the casual print handle. It’s time to give the workshop an identity that matches where we’re going.

Welcome to **Cold Front Forge**.

## Why Cold Front Forge?

The name pays homage to our home base in Buffalo, NY. Lake-effect squalls and winter cold fronts teach you to value tools and equipment that are dependable, rugged, and built without fluff.

Cold Front Forge combines that industrial work ethic with modern digital fabrication. We treat additive manufacturing like software engineering: code-driven models, reproducible tolerances, and modular designs.

## Our Three Core Product Lines

Rather than a scattered assortment of files, Cold Front Forge is focusing on three distinct categories:

1. **Custom Trophies & Awards:** Bespoke championship belts, fantasy league awards, and regional tournament prizes designed to stand out on a mantle instead of looking like generic catalog plastic.
2. **Tabletop & TCG Organization:** Precision deck vaults, graded slab displays, and token counters designed specifically for card collectors and tabletop gamers.
3. **Home Gym Hardware & Tools:** Heavy-duty rack attachments, accessory pegs, and specialty barbell storage engineered to take real abuse.

## Keeping the Open-Source Spirit Intact

With this rebrand, we are formally opening our [Etsy storefront](https://coldfrontforge.etsy.com/) for those who want production-ready finished hardware delivered to their door.

However, our open-source commitment has not changed. The core parametric OpenSCAD scripts and ready-to-slice STL files will continue to be shared for free across our favorite maker communities:

- **Source Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/OneBuffaloLabs/ColdFrontForge)
- **Model Downloads:** Grab free print profiles on [MakerWorld](https://makerworld.com/en/@ColdFrontForge), [Printables](https://www.printables.com/@CFForge_4259106), [Thangs](https://thangs.com/designer/ColdFrontForge), [Thingiverse](https://www.thingiverse.com/coldfrontforge/designs), and [Creality Cloud](https://www.crealitycloud.com/user/2867011039/profile).

If you have a 3D printer, warm up the bed and download the files. If you need finished goods or a custom commercial run for your league or gym, you can [request custom work directly on our site](https://coldfrontforge.com/custom-requests/).

You can also follow along with our latest builds, prototypes, and print failures on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/coldfrontforge/), [Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/coldfrontforge.com), [X](https://x.com/ColdFrontForge), and [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/ColdFrontForge).
